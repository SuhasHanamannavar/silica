A modern semiconductor die is a vertically stacked system of extraordinary complexity. Beneath the surface, multiple layers of patterned metal interconnect form a dense transportation network, routing signals between billions of transistors arranged in standard-cell rows. Power distribution grids, clock trees, and I/O structures complete the architecture — each layer precisely engineered to meet timing, power, and signal integrity constraints.

— Layer Stack

A semiconductor die is composed of a complex series of stacked materials, each meticulously engineered to perform a specific function in the electronic system. At the foundation lies the silicon substrate, doped to create transistor channels. Above this, polysilicon gates and diffusion regions form the active devices. Multiple layers of metal interconnect — separated by dielectric — route signals vertically and horizontally. The uppermost layers carry global power and clock distribution, while a final passivation layer protects the delicate structure beneath.

Standard Cells

Pre-characterized logic gates (NAND, NOR, flip-flops) arranged in uniform-height rows, the fundamental building blocks of digital implementation.

I/O Structures

Perimeter circuits that translate internal voltage levels to external interface standards, with ESD protection and signal conditioning.

Clock Distribution

Balanced H-tree or mesh networks delivering the timing reference with controlled skew across billions of sequential elements.

Power Grid

Multi-layer mesh of wide metal straps distributing VDD and VSS with controlled IR drop and electromigration margins.

— Final Handoff

Tapeout
Preparation

Silica prepares the final design database for external manufacturing. The platform assembles the verified layout into industry-standard stream formats, generates all required signoff reports, and packages the complete handoff kit for foundry submission. Silica itself does not fabricate silicon wafers — manufacturing is performed by third-party foundry partners under their own process controls and quality standards.

GDSII Stream

Full hierarchical layout database in Caltech Intermediate Format derivative, ready for mask generation.

OASIS

Open Artwork System Interchange Standard — compressed layout representation for efficient foundry transfer.

Signoff Reports

Timing (STA), power, IR drop, electromigration, DRC, LVS, and antenna rule verification summaries.
