01 / Workflow

The design journey.

Ten disciplined stages from architecture to tapeout. Stages 01–04 (Specification to Logic Synthesis) are available now; stages 05–10 are coming soon.

01
Frontend● Available

Specification

Architecture, performance targets, power budget, and die-area envelope. The contract every downstream stage is measured against.

02
Frontend● Available

RTL Design

Behavioral description in hardware-description language — registers, datapaths, and finite state machines captured as synthesizable logic.

03
Frontend● Available

Functional Verification

Verilator C++ testbenches, assertions, and coverage closure prove the RTL does what the specification requires.

04
Frontend● Available

Logic Synthesis

Yosys logic synthesis mapping RTL to standard cells. Gates sized for timing, area, and cell count.

Floorplanning

Macro blocks, I/O rings, and power domains arranged on the die. The skeleton on which every later stage is built.

02 / Frontend Flow

From English to gates, verified.

Silica transforms a natural-language specification into synthesizable SystemVerilog, then proves it with Verilator C++ simulation before a single gate is placed.

  • Specification documents

    Ten formal deliverables: microarchitecture, interfaces, PPA targets, CSR map, verification plan, and SDC constraints.

  • Verilator-clean RTL

    Synthesizable SystemVerilog linted and compiled to a cycle-accurate C++ simulation model.

  • Functional verification closure

    Directed and constrained-random stimulus with assertion checking. Every artifact verified before synthesis.

03 / Die Anatomy

What's on the die.

Every region of the chip has a defined purpose. Silica's synthesis output maps cleanly onto the physical implementation that follows.

Standard Cells & Logic Gates
The bulk of the die is filled with rows of standard cells — NAND, NOR, flip-flops, and multiplexers drawn from the foundry library, placed in legal rows and interconnected by routed metal.

04 / Logic Synthesis

RTL becomes gates.

Yosys technology-maps the verified RTL onto a standard-cell library. The result is a gate-level netlist with real timing, area, and power numbers.

  • Gate-level netlist

    Structural Verilog composed entirely of standard-cell instances. Ready for physical design handoff.

  • Timing & area reports

    Setup slack, gate equivalents, and core cell area. Every number produced by the synthesis toolchain.

  • Design-rule clean

    Unmapped logic, constraint violations, and synthesis warnings all diagnosed and resolved by specialist agents.
